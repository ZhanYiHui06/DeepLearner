## What is it?

The DeepLearner Challenge is the club’s annual technical competition. We take meaningful problems from the real world as challenge topics, and teams use AI to deliver their own solutions **within a limited time**.

This isn’t a problem-grinding contest, nor a paper-reproduction exercise. What we value most is your complete thought process of **discovering, defining and solving a problem**.

## Eligibility &amp; Rules

- Open to all DeepLearner members, regardless of year or major
- 2–4 members per team; cross-year teams are welcome
- Topics usually cover NLP, CV, data analysis and similar areas
- Final submissions must include a runnable demo and a brief technical report
