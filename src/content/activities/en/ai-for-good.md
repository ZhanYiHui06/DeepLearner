## What is it?

“AI for Good” is DeepLearner’s flagship community outreach program. We take our members off campus into real-world settings to think about **how AI can serve everyday people**.

Whether it’s helping seniors get to know the AI features on their smartphones or setting up simple AI teaching demos for rural schools, we believe technology only matters **when people actually use it**.

## How to Get Involved

Before each outreach trip, we post a call for volunteers in our QQ group with the time, place and roles needed (speakers, teaching assistants, photographers and more). **You don’t need to be a tech wizard.** If you’re willing to step out and do some good, that’s enough.
