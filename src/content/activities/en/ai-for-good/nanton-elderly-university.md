# Nantong University for the Elderly

In May 2025, the DeepLearner team visited the Nantong University for the Elderly to run an AI outreach session for its senior students.

## What We Did

Our volunteers worked one-on-one with the students to show them how to use the AI features on their smartphones in everyday situations, including voice assistants, smart photography and voice input. We explained the basic ideas behind AI in plain language, so new technology no longer felt unfamiliar.

## Outcomes

- Demonstrated and taught AI features to more than 30 senior students
- Helped them get comfortable with practical tools such as voice assistants and smart translation
- Gathered genuine needs and feedback on AI technology from older adults
