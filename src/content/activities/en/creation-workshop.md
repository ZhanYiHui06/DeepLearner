## What is it?

“Creation Workshop” is DeepLearner’s hands-on workshop series, open to all students. We believe getting your hands dirty comes before creating anything, and that it calls for **a quiet space, reliable teammates and help at the right moment**.

Each session picks a trending technology application. With thorough explanations of the theory and step-by-step demos, every participant can bring an idea of their own to life in a short time.
